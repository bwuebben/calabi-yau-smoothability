# Partial smoothings of Calabi–Yau threefolds with degree-six del Pezzo cone points

Bernd Johannes Wuebben, 26 November 2026. [Manuscript](main.pdf) · [Source](main.tex) · [Submission source archive](submission-source.tar.gz).

Theorem 2.2 removes the nonzero-projection hypothesis for a selected local deformation component at every degree-six cone, under the resolution hypotheses on page 1. Theorem 3.1 describes the exact zero sets of the marked nodal periods. Proposition 4.1 identifies the integral cohomology of the regular locus and the restrictions to the remaining links. Section 5 gives two compact examples.

From this directory, build with `latexmk -pdf -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex`. Run the standalone integral calculations with `sage -c "load('check_examples.sage')"` (SageMath 10.9). The supplement includes the ordered lattice vertices, the marked faces, the relations, and the full integrality calculation. All TeX inputs are included here.
