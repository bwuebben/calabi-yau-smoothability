\section{Geometric realisation on the small resolution}\label{sec:resolution}

Theorem~\ref{thm:res} supplements the comparison of relation lattices with a geometric construction on
$\widehat X$. Proposition~\ref{prop:integral} already determines the integral relations among the exceptional curves.
Here a strict tropical $2$-cycle specifies a three-dimensional domain and a continuous map into $\widehat X$;
the degrees of its boundary spheres give the relation. Theorems~\ref{thm:main}, \ref{thm:real} and~\ref{thm:lift} and
Corollary~\ref{cor:smooth} are independent of this construction. Its complete proof is in Appendix~\ref{app:original}.

We retain the hypotheses of Theorem~\ref{thm:res}: $\Delta$ is admissible and satisfies~\ref{hyp:U}, the profile satisfies
\ref{hyp:P}, and $\widehat X\to X'_\sigma$ is a projective small resolution induced by a regular refinement of
$\Sigma'$. The diagonals of this resolution determine the signs of the node coefficients $\rho_q(S)$.
The exceptional curves $C_q$ have their complex orientations. The following notation describes the construction.
\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{p{2.7cm}p{10.3cm}}
object & meaning\\\hline
$\widehat B$, $\hat S$ & the resolved affine base and the cycle moved to it; a node becomes an edge $e_q$ between two negative vertices\\
$\mathscr W$ & a smooth localized hypersurface, isotopic to the algebraic small resolution\\
$\mathscr W_U$ & a region of $\mathscr W$ associated to a small adapted open set $U$ of the base\\
$\mathfrak D(S)$ & the circle bundle over the smooth sheets, completed at the edges, vertices and discriminant\\
$F$, $f_S$ & the maps of this domain to $\mathscr W$ and, after transport, to $\widehat X$\\
$\partial_q\mathfrak D$ & the boundary sphere over $e_q$, mapped to $C_q$ with degree $-\rho_q(S)$
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\subsection{The geometric statement}\label{ssec:res-outline}
The domain in Theorem~\ref{thm:res} is a three-dimensional CW complex, with an integral fundamental chain
$[\mathfrak D]$ given by its oriented top cells. Over the smooth sheets away from a neighbourhood of the discriminant,
its fibre is the circle $\R v/\Z v$, oriented by the primitive field $v$. At an interior edge the three circle fibres
are joined by the triangle prescribed by balancing; at an interior vertex the four triangles are filled by a tetrahedron.
The remaining local domains complete it near the discriminant (Definition~\ref{def:domain}). For each node met by
$j(\partial S)$, its distinguished boundary sphere is
\[
\partial_q\mathfrak D=(e_q\times S^1)/\!\sim\ \cong S^2,
\]
where the circle is collapsed at both endpoints of $e_q$. The continuous map $f_S\colon\mathfrak D(S)\to\widehat X$
satisfies
\[
\partial[\mathfrak D]=\sum_q[\partial_q\mathfrak D],\qquad
f_S(\partial_q\mathfrak D)\subset C_q,\qquad
\deg(f_S|_{\partial_q\mathfrak D})=-\rho_q(S).
\]
These properties are the geometric content of Theorem~\ref{thm:res}. For chosen fundamental singular cycles $[C_q]$,
the degree identity gives integral singular $3$-chains $A_q$ supported in $C_q$ with
\[
\partial A_q=(f_S)_*[\partial_q\mathfrak D]+\rho_q(S)[C_q].
\]
Consequently
\[
\Xi(S)=-(f_S)_*[\mathfrak D]+\sum_q A_q,\qquad
\partial\Xi(S)=\sum_q\rho_q(S)[C_q].
\]
The corrections turn equality of homology classes on the boundary curves into equality of singular chains.
The map and the chain depend on the auxiliary choices; independence of the resulting relative class is not asserted
(Remark~\ref{rem:res-choices}). For another small resolution, Lemma~\ref{lem:flop} gives a bounding chain with the
coefficient negated at each flopped curve, and the same relative class on the common exterior of the node
neighbourhoods. This comparison concerns chains and their relative classes.

\subsection{Mechanisms of the construction}
Casta\~no-Bernard and Matessi construct a completed circle bundle in their topological resolution models
\cite[\S7.5, \S7.7]{CBM}. We construct a map of this domain into a localized hypersurface $\mathscr W$ and then into
$\widehat X$. The localized equation is obtained by cutting off monomials far from the maximum, following
\cite{Mikhalkin,Ya16}. It has exact local equations near the relevant toric strata, a positive real section, and
aspherical regions $\mathscr W_U$ away from the discriminant. Their fundamental groups identify with the integral
tangent lattices of $\widehat B$, compatibly with parallel transport (Lemma~\ref{lem:regions}).

Four local arguments are needed to realize the circle domain in this model.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \emph{Collar paths through real chambers.} The positive real section avoids every toric stratum, whereas the
circle of $v$ collapses only on a stratum. A collar path joins the section to the critical locus of the local model,
turning the phase of one character by $\pi$ (Definition~\ref{def:collarpath}). Sweeping it by the circle of $v$ gives
the lift near a boundary leg. Lemma~\ref{lem:chamber} identifies the real chambers through which these paths run.
\item \emph{Phase corrections at positive vertices.} To join neighbouring collar paths by a disc, their boundary loop
must have zero total winding in certain characters (Lemma~\ref{lem:pi1}). This holds directly at nodes and negative
vertices. At a positive vertex it is achieved by rotating the paths along the incident legs by an integral multiple of $\pi$ in
a circle action that preserves $\mathscr W$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:collar}(3)).
\item \emph{Discs at changes of side.} When a sheet changes side of a two-face, an explicit switching disc joins the
two families of collar paths (Lemma~\ref{lem:collar}(4)). It supplies the compatibility needed to glue the local maps.
\item \emph{Signed node degrees.} The node map sweeps an arc between the two poles of the exceptional curve by the
circle of the boundary field. The circle action and the orientation of the arc give degree $-\rho_q(S)$
(Lemma~\ref{lem:nodedeg}). Both the global minus sign in $\Xi(S)$ and the change of coefficient under a flop follow
from this calculation and the comparison of the two resolutions of the node.
\end{enumerate}

The local maps extend over the regular part cell by cell. A circle fibre represents $v$ in
$\pi_1(\mathscr W_U)$; balancing makes the boundaries of the triangle pieces null-homotopic, and asphericity permits
the higher extensions (Lemma~\ref{lem:ext}). This is a local homotopy description of the map, without requiring a
global torus fibration of $\widehat X$. Finally an orbit-preserving ambient isotopy carries $\mathscr W$ to $\widehat X$
and its exceptional curves to the $C_q$ with degree one (Lemmas~\ref{lem:T0} and~\ref{lem:T}). Its composition with
$F$ is $f_S$.

The construction uses the whole strict cycle and its balancing data. It does not use a previously known relation
among the $[C_q]$ to choose a bounding chain. On the mirror, Theorem~\ref{thm:lift} instead realizes the same coefficients
by a $4$-chain whose regular part is, locally up to homotopy, the bundle of two-tori annihilated by $v$. The two constructions therefore give geometric
representatives of the corresponding relations in different dimensions.

\subsection{The example \texorpdfstring{$X_9$}{X9}}\label{ssec:res-x9}
The example of Section~\ref{ssec:x9} has four nodes and
\[
K=\Z(1,-1,-1,1).
\]
For the triangulation and base in the accompanying data, Lemma~\ref{lem:x9belt} proves that the belt disc $S$ of
Figure~\ref{fig:x9}, with constant primitive field $v=-d_0$, is a strict tropical $2$-cycle. Its boundary runs along
$18$ legs through the four nodes and five positive vertices; its interior misses the discriminant. The node
coefficients are $(1,-1,-1,1)$.

Here the regular part of $\mathfrak D(S)$ is a circle bundle over the disc. The local construction collapses the
circle along the boundary legs, makes the phase corrections at the positive vertices, and leaves four boundary
spheres, of degrees $(-1,1,1,-1)$ on $(C_{q_1},C_{q_2},C_{q_3},C_{q_4})$. Thus
\[
\partial\Xi(S)=[C_{q_1}]-[C_{q_2}]-[C_{q_3}]+[C_{q_4}].
\]
The mirror lift of the same disc has
\[
\partial\check\Xi(S)=[\delta_{q_1}]-[\delta_{q_2}]-[\delta_{q_3}]+[\delta_{q_4}].
\]
The first identity is realized on the original small resolution $\widehat X_9$, and the second on the mirror
hypersurface $Y_t$. The polytope, belt, and verification of strictness are given in Section~\ref{ssec:x9}; the full
resolution construction applies to this disc without additional hypotheses.
