For a class of Calabi--Yau hypersurfaces in toric fourfolds, and for suitable members of the Batyrev mirror family near
the maximally degenerate limit, we prove that the exceptional curves of a small resolution and the mirror vanishing
three-spheres have the same integral relation lattice, and that the spheres generate a torsion-free subgroup. Every
relation is realised by an integral four-chain on the mirror hypersurface, constructed from a tropical two-cycle,
relative to the discriminant, on the base of Gross's toric degeneration. Near these members the locus where all the
spheres collapse is smooth of codimension equal to the relative Picard number; this establishes, locally in the
coefficient space of the mirror family, Morrison's prediction that the mirror nodes impose exactly that number of
conditions. We characterise smoothability by relations among the mirror spheres and obtain a symplectic conifold
transition on the mirror whenever a smoothing exists. The results apply to nodal crepant partial resolutions,
induced by projective toric morphisms, of anticanonical hypersurfaces whose singular points are nodes and cones over
del Pezzo surfaces of degrees six and seven, one on each singular two-face of the polytope. The unimodular
triangulations needed for Gross's construction are verified to exist by computer for the $51{,}827$ such polytopes
identified in the Kreuzer--Skarke list.
