Let $X$ be an anticanonical hypersurface in the toric variety of a reflexive four-polytope whose singularities are
isolated, at most one on each torus orbit, and are ordinary double points and cones over del Pezzo surfaces of degrees
seven and six. Let $X'$ be a crepant partial resolution with only nodes that is induced by a projective toric
morphism. Let $K$ be the lattice of relations among
the exceptional curves of a small resolution of $X'$. We prove that on the Batyrev mirror family, near its maximally
degenerate limit, the vanishing spheres $\delta_q$ of the critical points corresponding to the nodes satisfy exactly the
relations in $K$: an integral combination $\sum_qk_q[\delta_q]$ is zero, or even torsion, in $H_3$ if and only if $k\in K$.
The members of the mirror family with a node at each of these points form a smooth locus whose codimension, the number
of nodes minus the rank of $K$, is the relative Picard number of the small resolution, as Morrison's description of conifold
transitions under mirror symmetry predicts. Consequently $X'$ is smoothable if and only if the vanishing spheres satisfy
a relation with every coefficient nonzero. Each relation is realised by an explicit integral $4$-chain lifted from a
relative tropical $2$-cycle on the base of Gross's toric degeneration of $X'$; that the node coefficients of these cycles
generate $K$ is proved by a computation of the homology of the complement of the discriminant with coefficients
in the local system of integral tangent vectors. For the tropical
$2$-cycles of Casta\~no-Bernard and Matessi we also construct the corresponding $3$-chains on the small resolution, so
that one tropical object bounds on both sides of the transition. A combinatorial condition used in the construction is
verified for all admissible polytopes by a computer-assisted check of the Kreuzer--Skarke list.
