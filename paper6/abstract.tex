We establish and strengthen Morrison's numerical prediction for conifold transitions under mirror symmetry
for a class of toric Calabi--Yau threefolds. For suitable degenerations of the Batyrev mirror family near
the maximally degenerate limit, we prove that the exceptional curves of a small resolution and the mirror
vanishing three-spheres have the same integral relation lattice, and that the spheres generate a torsion-free
subgroup. Every relation is realised by an explicit integral four-chain on the mirror hypersurface,
constructed from a relative tropical two-cycle. The mirror nodal locus is smooth of codimension equal to
the relative Picard number. We characterise smoothability by relations among the mirror spheres and obtain
a symplectic conifold transition on the mirror whenever a smoothing exists. The results apply to nodal
crepant partial resolutions, induced by projective toric morphisms, of a specified class of anticanonical
hypersurfaces with nodes and cones over del Pezzo surfaces of degrees six and seven. The required
triangulations are established by computer-assisted verification.
